Bin 02 · Core principles

Six of the nine, as practiced

The founder listed nine principles. These are the ones I actually use at work.

I

Verve

Building teams and programs from zero to N — greenfield or inherited — is the part of the job that energizes me most.

II

Vision

Yearly roadmaps, quarterly plans, honest forecasts. SAFe and PI Planning when the scale calls for it.

III

Benevolence

People first. A sustainable pace and team morale are delivery metrics, not extras.

IV

Probity

Estimates belong to the people doing the work. Risks and dependencies go on the table early.

V

Nimbleness

Handovers across borders — London, Norway, India — without dropping delivery on the floor.

VI

Humility

I mentor people into roles I used to hold. A team that doesn’t need me is a finished job.
